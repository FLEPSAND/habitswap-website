About

An app for people
who want a fresh start

Habit Swap is made by Andreas Fleps in Möckmühl, Germany, as an independent project with no investors and no ads.

Why Habit Swap exists

Many habit apps count days and reward streaks. Slip once and you’re back at zero, feeling worse than before. Habit Swap is meant to be different: honest about what’s hard, practical in the moment that matters, and full of hope, because change doesn’t depend on our strength alone.

The app brings together what research knows about habits and what the Christian faith says about repentance, grace, and community. Together they carry further than either on its own.

Principles

What we stand by

Honest, not shaming

We talk plainly about pornography, alcohol, and other struggles, without wagging fingers. No one should feel worse after opening the app.

Grace, not pressure

A slip isn’t a fall off the mountain. Whoever falls can get up and keep going, today.

Your data is yours

What you record in the app stays on your iPhone. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

Faith as an invitation

Habit Swap is shaped by Christian faith and open to everyone. No one has to believe to use it.

Grounded, not sensational

Our articles rely on established research and name their sources. Where something is unclear, we say so.

Not a substitute for help

The app doesn’t replace a doctor, a therapist, or addiction counseling. In a crisis, it shows you where to get help right away.
