Habit Swap

Replace bad habits with good ones – a little higher every day.

Download on the App Store

Swap instead of struggle

If you only suppress a habit, you fight the same gap every day. That’s why Habit Swap is built around a set swap: you decide in advance what you’ll do when the urge comes, and when it usually comes. So in the moment that matters, you have a plan, not just a rule.

Emergency coach

When the urge gets strong, open the coach. It asks, calms you down, and reminds you of your swap. The coach and the crisis card are always free.

90-day climb

Every day takes you higher, from base camp to the summit cross. A slip doesn’t throw you back into the valley; you learn from it and keep going.

Daily check-in

A short look back each evening: how was your day, what triggered you, what helped? Over time you start to see your patterns.

Not alone

Connect with an accountability partner or a private prayer group. You decide what you share.

Metanoia – change that comes from within

We believe real change doesn’t begin with more willpower, but with a new heart. If you like, the app walks with you through God’s word, prayer, and people who pray for you. The app is open to everyone – whether you already know Jesus, want to get to know him, or simply want a fresh start.

What Habit Swap helps with

Quit: pornography, alcohol, smoking and vaping, cannabis, gambling and sports betting, gaming, social media and phone use, sugar and fast food, procrastination.
Build: exercise, getting up early, reading, prayer and Bible reading, healthy eating. Or something entirely your own.

Your data stays with you

Your progress, your check-ins, and your notes are stored only on your device. No ads, no tracking. Only if you use “Together” is the data you choose to share stored, encrypted, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Habit Swap cost?

You can try Habit Swap free for 3 days. After that, Habit Swap Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription; the App Store shows the price in your local currency. The emergency coach and the crisis card are always free. You can cancel anytime in your iPhone settings.

Do I have to be a Christian to use Habit Swap?

No. Habit Swap is open to everyone. When you start, you choose whether you already believe in Jesus, want to get to know him, or would rather leave faith out for now. Bible verses, prayer, and prayer groups are an invitation, not a requirement.

Which habits does Habit Swap help with?

To quit: pornography, alcohol, smoking and vaping, cannabis, gambling and sports betting, gaming, social media and phone use, sugar and fast food, procrastination, or something of your own. To build: exercise, getting up early, reading, prayer and Bible reading, healthy eating, or something of your own.

What happens to my data?

Your progress, check-ins, and notes stay on your iPhone. No ads, no tracking. Only if you use “Together” is the data you choose to share stored, encrypted, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.

Is Habit Swap available for Android?

For now, Habit Swap is available for iPhone only.
