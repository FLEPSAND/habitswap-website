How it works

Willpower alone rarely lasts.
A good swap usually does.

If you only suppress a habit, you fight the same gap every day. Habit Swap fills that gap with something good.

Spot your triggers

Note when the urge usually comes: the time, the place, the mood. Every check-in makes your patterns clearer.

Set your swap

Decide in advance what you’ll do instead in exactly that moment. When it gets hard, the emergency coach reminds you.

Climb every day

Every day earns elevation, from base camp to the summit cross. A slip doesn’t send you back to the valley.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does Habit Swap cost?

You can try Habit Swap free for 3 days. After that, Habit Swap Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription; the App Store shows the price in your local currency. The emergency coach and the crisis card are always free. You can cancel anytime in your iPhone settings.

Do I have to be a Christian to use Habit Swap?

No. Habit Swap is open to everyone. When you start, you choose whether you already believe in Jesus, want to get to know him, or would rather leave faith out for now. Bible verses, prayer, and prayer groups are an invitation, not a requirement.

Which habits does Habit Swap help with?

To quit: pornography, alcohol, smoking and vaping, cannabis, gambling and sports betting, gaming, social media and phone use, sugar and fast food, procrastination, or something of your own. To build: exercise, getting up early, reading, prayer and Bible reading, healthy eating, or something of your own.

What happens to my data?

Your progress, check-ins, and notes stay on your iPhone. No ads, no tracking. Only if you use “Together” is the data you choose to share stored, encrypted, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.

Is Habit Swap available for Android?

For now, Habit Swap is available for iPhone only.
