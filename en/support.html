Help

Contact

Questions, bugs, or wishes? Write to kontakt@habitswap.de. We’ll reply as soon as we can.

Do I need an account?

No. Goals, check-ins, the coach, and the articles work without an account, and everything stays on your device. You only need an account (with Apple or Google) for “Together”, that is, for partners and groups.

How do I invite a partner?

Open “Together”, sign in, and tap “Invite a partner”. Give the code to the other person. It’s valid for seven days.

What do my partners see?

Only what you choose to share. By default, that’s your days free and your streak. You only share slips and mood if you turn them on.

How do I report a post?

Tap “Report” on a post or reply. You can also block people. We review reported content within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under “Together” → “Account” → “Delete account”. Everything you’ve shared will then be deleted.

In a crisis

Habit Swap does not replace medical, psychological, or pastoral care. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number now. In the US call or text 988, in the UK and Ireland call Samaritans on 116 123. More: findahelpline.com.
