1. What this is about

Habit Swap is an app by Andreas Fleps that helps you replace bad habits with good ones. After a free trial of 3 days, using the app requires a subscription (section 6). Most features run only on your device. For “Together” (partners and groups) you need an account. By creating an account, you agree to these terms.

In groups and in exchanges with partners, we treat each other with respect. In particular, the following is not allowed:

  • abusive, degrading, threatening, or hateful content,
  • sexual or pornographic content and links to it,
  • depictions of violence and calls for violence or self-harm,
  • advertising, spam, and fraud,
  • publishing other people’s personal data without their consent,

There is zero tolerance for such content.

4. Reporting and blocking

You can report any post and any reply in the app, and you can block any person. We review reported content within 24 hours. We remove content that violates these rules, and anyone who violates them can be permanently excluded from “Together”.

5. Your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under “Together” → “Account”. All data you have shared is then deleted. Habit Swap is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Habit Swap Premium is available as a monthly subscription and as a yearly subscription. The current price in your currency is shown in the App Store before you buy. You can cancel or switch at any time in your Apple Account settings under “Subscriptions” or in the app under Profile → “Manage subscription”; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
